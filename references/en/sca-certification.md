# SCA Certification Landscape

> A systematic map of the SCA Coffee Skills Program (CSP) six-module course tree + the independent Q-Grader certification (from 2025, Evolved Q Grader, built on CVA).
>
> **Beginner wording**: SCA is the Specialty Coffee Association. It has 6 courses plus one Q-Grader (coffee quality grader) certification. Courses teach skills; Q-Grader tests your tasting/grading ability.

## 0. Why certifications matter

The SCA (Specialty Coffee Association) certification is the international competency anchor of the specialty-coffee industry. The CSP course system covers the full chain of skills from entry to professional; the Q-Grader is the highest certification for coffee quality grading.

**CSP vs Q-Grader**:
- CSP = Coffee Skills Program, a **course system** (complete it to get a certificate)
- Q-Grader = coffee quality grader certification (pass the exam to get a license), independent of CSP

## 1. SCA Coffee Skills Program (CSP) -- six modules

| Module | Level | Suggested hours | For whom |
|--------|-------|-----------------|----------|
| Introduction to Coffee | Foundation (single level) | 6h | all beginners |
| Barista Skills | F / I / P | 6 / 14 / 21h | bar counter operators |
| Brewing | F / I / P | 6 / 14 / 21h | brewing enthusiasts |
| Sensory Skills | F / I / P | 6 / 14 / 21h | those building tasting skill |
| Roasting | F / I / P | 6 / 14 / 21h | roasting hobbyists |
| Green Coffee | F / I / P | 6 / 14 / 21h | sourcing & QC |

**Prerequisites**: Intermediate requires completing Introduction to Coffee first; Professional requires completing the same module's Intermediate first.

## 2. Q-Grader certification (independent of CSP)

- **History**: originally issued by CQI (Coffee Quality Institute); from 2025 SCA took it over as Evolved Q Grader
- **Built on CVA**: the new Q-Grader is built on the four CVA tables (SCA-102~105), replacing the old 100-point cupping system
- **8 exam categories**: total 20-22 units (see [qgrader-complete-guide.md](qgrader-complete-guide.md))
- **Validity**: 3 years, requires re-certification
- **Global holders**: ~4000 people (2024-2025 data)

## 3. Relationship between CSP and Q-Grader

- CSP Sensory Skills Professional is the **recommended prerequisite** for Q-Grader (not mandatory)
- Q-Grader **is not part of** the six CSP modules; it is an independent certification
- Certificate combination suggestions:
  - Enthusiast: CSP Brewing + Sensory Skills (Foundation/Intermediate)
  - Professional: all CSP modules + Q-Grader
